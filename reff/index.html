Reff

v1.0 · macOS · 306 KB

Reff puts time-synced Spotify lyrics on your MacBook Touch Bar. It sits in the Control Strip, so it stays there no matter which app you're in — tap it, and the current line follows along with whatever's playing.

It's a menu bar app with no window and no Dock icon. No account, no sign-in, nothing to configure.

Download for macOS

Free and open source. Requires a Mac with a Touch Bar.

Requirements

  • 01 A Mac with a Touch Bar 2016–2023 models
  • 02 macOS 12 or later Monterey and up
  • 03 Spotify desktop app not the web player

Apple dropped the Touch Bar from the 14″ and 16″ MacBook Pro in 2021, and the last Touch Bar model was discontinued in 2023. If your Mac has function keys, Reff has nowhere to draw.

Installing

  1. 1 Open the downloaded .dmg and drag Reff into your Applications folder.
  2. 2 The first launch will be blocked — see below. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway.
  3. 3 macOS will ask to let Reff control Spotify. Click OK — this is how it reads the current track.
  4. 4 Play something, then tap the Reff icon in the Control Strip on the right of your Touch Bar.

Why macOS blocks it

Reff isn't notarized by Apple, so the first launch shows “Apple could not verify Reff is free of malware.”

Notarizing needs a paid Apple Developer account, which this little app doesn't have yet. The Open Anyway step above is the way through, and you only do it once.

Prefer the terminal? This strips the download quarantine flag instead: 

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Reff.app

Don't take my word for it — the source is on GitHub if you'd rather read it and build it yourself.

How it works

  • 01 Reads Spotify locally AppleScript
  • 02 Fetches synced lyrics lrclib.net
  • 03 Lives in the Control Strip always visible

Track name, artist, and duration are sent to lrclib.net to look up lyrics. Nothing else leaves your Mac — no account, no analytics, no listening history.
